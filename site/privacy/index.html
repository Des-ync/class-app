ShuttlePass Demo privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

ShuttlePass Demo is a demonstration app. It shows how a subscription shuttle service could work, using sample routes, sample drivers and simulated payments. It does not provide a real transport service.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

If you create a local profile, the name, email address and rider type you enter are saved on your device, together with your chosen route and stop, sample subscription, sample receipts and simulated trip history. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share a receipt using your device's share sheet.

The checkout is simulated. It uses fixed test details, never asks for a real card or mobile money number, and charges nothing.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Deleting your data

Open Profile and choose Delete local data, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything ShuttlePass Demo stored. There is no other copy to delete.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's store listings will be updated to match.
