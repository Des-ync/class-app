Delete your data

ShuttlePass Demo stores your local profile, route, sample receipts and trip history only on your phone. We hold no account or copy on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

In the app

Open Profile, choose Delete local data and confirm. Everything the app stored is removed immediately.

By uninstalling

Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your phone.
