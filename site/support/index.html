Support

Email kingema93@gmail.com with your phone model, the app version (shown in your app store) and what happened. We aim to reply within a few working days.

Is this a real shuttle service?

No. ShuttlePass Demo is a demonstration. Routes, drivers, payments and tracking are simulated, and no real rides are provided.

Was I charged?

No. The checkout is simulated, uses fixed test details and never asks for real payment information. Receipts in the app are samples.

How do I try the different views?

On the sign-in screen, choose Login as Student, Login as Worker or Login as Driver. Profile lets you switch between the commuter, driver and admin views.

Something looks stuck

Close and reopen the app. If it still misbehaves, open Profile and choose Delete local data to start fresh.
