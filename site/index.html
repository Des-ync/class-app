SHUTTLEPASS DEMO

ShuttlePass Demo

An interactive demo of a subscription shuttle service for school and work commutes in Accra, Ghana. Pick a route, try a monthly plan, show a QR boarding pass, follow the shuttle on a map, and see the driver and operator views.

Everything is simulated and stays on your phone. There are no real rides, no real payments and no accounts on a server.
